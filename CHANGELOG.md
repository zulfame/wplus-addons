# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - 2026-10-26

- New: English and Indonesian texts; the plugin follows the language of each user.
- New: the settings page (Settings > WPlus Addons) with a switch for each tool.
- New: Tools > Thumbnail. Make a cover picture with three layouts (Minimal with a thin frame, Dots with a 3D dotted ring, Panel), left, center or right text, light or dark, your own theme color, logo, organization and two tags, four sizes and three fonts, and download it as WebP (the default, lighter and better for SEO) or PNG. Drawn in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.
- New: Save to Media Library. The picture gets its title, alt text and description from your fields, and only people who may upload files can save.
- New: a Bulk tab under Tools > Thumbnail. It lists the posts that have no featured image; tick the ones you want and each gets its own picture, one after the other, with a progress bar and a Stop button. A post that already has a featured image is never changed.
- New: presets. Save your design choices (layout, color, mode, font, size, logo, organization) under a name, make one the starting point, and pick them in the editor panel too. Presets are shared by the whole site and only administrators can change them. The starting color follows WPlus UI when that theme is active.
- New: a Thumbnail panel in the post editor (Post tab). It fills itself from the title, excerpt, tags and your site, shows a preview, and one button makes the picture, saves it with the post and sets it as the featured image.
